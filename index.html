Stop losing jobs to voicemail.

I run an HVAC company in Chicago. In July our phones ring nonstop, and every call we miss usually goes to the next guy on Google. So I built a phone line that picks up when we can't.

It answers like a person at your front desk, gets the name, address and what's wrong, books the visit, and texts the customer a confirmation. If it's a no-heat or a burst pipe, it calls your on-call tech right away.

Try it free for 7 days

What it does

  • Picks up 24/7, including nights and weekends
  • Asks the questions your dispatcher would ask (system type, age, symptoms, access)
  • Books into your calendar or sends you the job as a text/email
  • Emergencies go straight to the on-call phone
  • Texts back anyone who hangs up before it answers
  • Every call recorded and summarized, so you can check how it did

The code is public if you want to see how it works: github.com/manno1o/contractor-voice-receptionist. I also share free n8n workflows for contractors here.
